## Direction contract

THESIS: Walid's work as a stack of printed film positives registered to one grid: name, product and proof are separate sheets that slide apart as you scroll. It refuses the engineer-portfolio default of a fixed sidebar beside a column of same-size project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Light-table film base (pale grey-green), black halftone ink, two spot inks: teal for Walid, tram yellow for Dowse's found results. Halftone dot screens, crop and registration marks at every sheet corner, translucent films that multiply where they overlap. Text always sits on clean achromatic paper slips; colour lives on films and edges. One condensed grotesk family carries display and body; a mono only for tokens, dates and measurements. Only the section in focus carries full spot ink; the rest of the stack sits in grey.

STORY: In one screen the visitor learns who Walid is and sees Dowse find a photo by description. Scrolling separates the films into Dowse's numbers, then four more pieces of work, the videos, the writing and the jobs. They leave knowing he ships local AI and explains LLMs, and they open LinkedIn.
