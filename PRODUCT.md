## Product Principles

1. Shipped work first. Every claim links to a repository, model, video or article.
2. Real numbers instead of adjectives, and only numbers with a source.
3. LinkedIn is one click away from anywhere on the site.
4. Stays current without hand edits: videos, articles and stars refresh on their own.
5. The visual ambition never costs speed or access: fast on phones, and still motion turned off for visitors who ask for reduced motion.
